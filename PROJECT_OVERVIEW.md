# Project Overview

## 1. Problem statement

The project analyzes a financial sales dataset to understand business performance and identify profitability risks.

The analysis considers:

- Sales
- Profit
- Profit margin
- Discounts
- Units sold
- Sale price
- Gross sales
- COGS
- Country
- Product
- Segment
- Discount band
- Time

The project deliberately moves from broad descriptive analysis to focused diagnostic analysis.

---

## 2. Analytical objective

The project was designed around four levels of questions.

### Level 1 — What is happening?

Understand:

- total sales
- total profit
- overall profit margin
- total discounts
- units sold
- sales and profit trends
- country performance
- product performance
- segment performance
- discount-band performance

### Level 2 — Where is the concern?

Identify segments, countries or products that show weak profitability.

### Level 3 — Why might profitability be weak?

Investigate:

- discount bands
- effective discount rate
- product-level profitability
- country-level profitability
- actual profit rather than margin alone

### Level 4 — What should management investigate?

Convert the evidence into practical recommendations without overstating causality.

---

## 3. Why the report uses three pages

### Page 1 — Executive Overview

Purpose: provide a broad business overview.

It answers:

> What is happening?

### Page 2 — Profitability Deep Dive

Purpose: investigate the profitability issue identified in the overview.

It answers:

> Where and under what conditions is profitability weak?

### Page 3 — Key Insights & Recommendations

Purpose: communicate the conclusion to a decision-maker.

It answers:

> So what?

This structure prevents the dashboard from becoming a collection of unrelated visuals.

---

## 4. Analytical storyline

The investigation developed in the following sequence:

1. Establish overall performance.
2. Compare performance across countries.
3. Compare products.
4. Compare segments.
5. Examine discount bands.
6. Identify Enterprise as the major profitability concern.
7. Test whether Enterprise weakness is geographically isolated.
8. Test whether Enterprise weakness is limited to one product.
9. Compare Enterprise margin across discount bands.
10. Compare actual Enterprise profit across discount bands.
11. Compare Enterprise sales volume across discount bands.
12. Calculate effective discount rate.
13. Drill down to Product × Discount Band.
14. Identify Carretera as the weakest Enterprise product.
15. Identify Montana as the strongest Enterprise product.
16. Convert findings into recommendations.

---

## 5. Core analytical principle

The project does not treat every visual as an end in itself.

Each major visual was created to answer a question.

That is the central analytical discipline used throughout the project:

**Question → Measure → Visual → Observation → Next question → Conclusion**
