# 07 - Page 2: Profitability Deep Dive

## Purpose

Page 2 answers:

> **Why does profitability require attention?**

It is the diagnostic page.

---

## Filters

The page uses:

- Segment
- Country
- Product
- Discount Band

The Segment filter is particularly important because selecting:

`Enterprise`

turns the page into a focused Enterprise investigation.

---

## KPI cards

The profitability page uses:

1. Total Sales
2. Total Profit
3. Profit Margin %
4. Total Discounts

Units Sold was intentionally removed from this page because profitability-focused KPIs provide a stronger analytical focus.

---

## Visual 1 - Segment × Discount Band Profit Margin Matrix

Rows:

`Segment`

Columns:

`Discount Band`

Values:

`Profit Margin %`


The Enterprise row is the key diagnostic pattern:

- None: +4.00%
- Low: +1.58%
- Medium: −3.03%
- High: −9.53%

---

## Visual 2 - Profit Margin by Product

Purpose:

> Determine whether Enterprise weakness is concentrated in a specific product.

With Enterprise selected:

**all six products are negative.**

This means the Enterprise issue is broader than one product.

Carretera is still the weakest product by total profit.

---

## Visual 3 - Profit Margin by Country

Purpose:

> Determine whether Enterprise weakness is geographically isolated.

With Enterprise selected, the countries analyzed remain negative.

Conclusion:

> The problem is not isolated to one country.

---

## Visual 4 - Enterprise Profit by Discount Band

This visual uses:

- Discount Band → X-axis
- Total Profit → Y-axis

Purpose:

> Move from percentage margin to actual financial impact.

The result:

- None → positive
- Low → positive
- Medium → negative
- High → largest loss

This confirms that the margin deterioration corresponds to actual profit deterioration.

---

## Visual 5 - Enterprise Sales by Discount Band

Fields:

- Discount Band → X-axis
- Total Sales → Y-axis

High-discount Enterprise sales are approximately:

**6.5M**

This is the largest Enterprise sales bucket among the discount bands analyzed.

This matters because the largest losses occur in a category that also represents substantial revenue.

---

## Visual 6 - Discount Rate by Discount Band

Measure:

`Total Discounts ÷ Total Gross Sales`

Approximate Enterprise results:

- None: 0%
- Low: 2.4%
- Medium: 6.8%
- High: 12.5%

This provides quantitative context for the discount-band categories.

---

## Visual 7 - Product × Discount Band Profit Matrix

Rows:

`Product`

Columns:

`Discount Band`

Values:

`Total Profit`

Conditional formatting highlights negative values.

Key observation:

- Carretera has the lowest total profit.
- Carretera's largest identified loss occurs at High discount.
- Montana has the highest total profit among Enterprise products.

---

## Diagnostic conclusion

The evidence supports the following statement:

> Enterprise has a broad profitability problem across products and countries. Profitability declines sharply as discount intensity increases, and high-discount Enterprise sales represent a substantial amount of revenue while generating the largest losses.

The evidence does not prove that discounting alone causes the losses.
