# 08 — Key Insights & Recommendations

## Executive Finding

**Enterprise is the primary profitability concern, with a −3.13% profit margin. The issue is broad across products and countries, while profitability declines sharply as discount intensity increases.**

---

## Key Findings

### Enterprise profitability

Enterprise has a **−3.13% profit margin**.

### Portfolio-wide weakness

All **six Enterprise products** have negative profit margins.

### Geographic breadth

Enterprise remains unprofitable across the countries analyzed.

### Discount relationship

Enterprise margin falls from:

**+4.00% → +1.58% → −3.03% → −9.53%**

as discount intensity increases from None → Low → Medium → High.

### High-discount exposure

High-discount Enterprise sales are approximately **6.5M**.

### Weakest product

**Carretera** has the lowest total profit among Enterprise products.

### Strongest product

**Montana** has the highest total profit among Enterprise products.

### Most concerning combination

**Enterprise + Carretera + High discount** is the most concerning identified product/discount combination.

---

# Recommendations

## 1. Review high-discount Enterprise transactions

Investigate heavily discounted Enterprise transactions where profitability becomes negative.

## 2. Reassess Carretera

Review Carretera's:

- price
- manufacturing cost
- discount level
- realized sales
- profit contribution

## 3. Introduce profitability checks

Consider discount approval thresholds or profitability checks for heavily discounted transactions.

## 4. Use Montana as an internal benchmark

Investigate why Montana produces stronger Enterprise economics.

## 5. Conduct deeper root-cause analysis

The observed relationship between discount intensity and profitability should be investigated at transaction level before claiming causality.

---

# Analytical conclusion

**Higher discount intensity is strongly associated with declining Enterprise profitability. The problem is not isolated to one product or country, making discount strategy and product economics the key areas for further investigation.**

---

# Important wording rule

Use:

> **strongly associated with**

rather than:

> **caused by**

The analysis establishes a relationship but does not independently establish causality.
