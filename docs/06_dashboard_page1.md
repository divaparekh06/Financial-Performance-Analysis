# 06 - Page 1: Executive Overview

## Purpose

Page 1 is the report's executive overview.

Its job is to answer:

> **What is happening in the business?**

It is intentionally broad rather than diagnostic.

---

## KPI cards

The page contains:

1. Total Sales
2. Total Profit
3. Profit Margin %
4. Total Discount
5. Total Units Sold

With all filters cleared, approximate values are:

- Sales: 118.73M
- Profit: 16.89M
- Margin: 14.23%
- Discounts: 9.21M
- Units Sold: 1.13M

---

## Slicers

Four slicers provide interactive filtering:

- Country
- Segment
- Product
- Discount Band

The slicers were tested individually and in combination.

Example:

`Country = France`

and

`Segment = Enterprise`

filters the entire analytical page to the selected context.

---

## Visual 1 - Sales & Profit Trend

Purpose:

> Understand how financial performance changes over time.

Observation:

- Sales generally trend upward over the period.
- Sales contain sharp peaks and drops.
- Profit is comparatively more stable, although it also contains fluctuations.

Analytical lesson:

> Revenue growth should not be treated as a direct proxy for profit growth.

---

## Visual 2 - Sales & Profit by Country

Purpose:

> Compare geographic contribution.

This visual supports questions such as:

- Which countries contribute substantial sales?
- Which countries generate stronger profit?
- Does sales leadership align with profit leadership?

---

## Visual 3 - Profit Margin by Segment

Purpose:

> Compare profitability across business segments.

The major anomaly is Enterprise.

Enterprise is the only segment identified with an overall negative profit margin in the analysis.

The visual uses red to emphasize the negative Enterprise result.

---

## Visual 4 - Sales & Profit by Product

Purpose:

> Compare product contribution to sales and profit.

This visual helps distinguish high-sales products from high-profit products.

---

## Visual 5 - Profit Margin by Discount Band

Purpose:

> Show how profitability changes across discount intensity.

The overall pattern shows declining margin as discount intensity increases.

This became the starting point for the Enterprise profitability investigation on Page 2.

---


## Page 1 analytical role

Page 1 is not intended to prove a root cause.

It identifies the anomaly and creates the reason to investigate it.

The key transition is:

**Overview → Enterprise anomaly → Profitability Deep Dive**
