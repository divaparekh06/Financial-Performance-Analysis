# 11 - Methodology and Limitations

## Methodology

The project follows a descriptive-to-diagnostic analytical approach.

### Stage 1 - Understand

Identify field meanings and aggregation behavior.

### Stage 2 - Validate

Cross-check Power BI calculations against Excel.

### Stage 3 - Explore

Examine time, country, product, segment and discount patterns.

### Stage 4 - Identify anomaly

Enterprise emerges as the major profitability concern.

### Stage 5 - Drill down

Investigate Enterprise by:

- product
- country
- discount band
- actual profit
- sales volume
- effective discount rate

### Stage 6 - Synthesize

Identify Carretera and high discounts as priority areas.

### Stage 7 - Recommend

Create evidence-based actions without overstating causality.

---

# Limitations

## 1. Association is not causation

The observed relationship between discount intensity and lower profitability does not prove that discounts alone cause losses.

---

## 2. Dataset-level analysis

The dashboard is primarily an aggregated analytical report.

It does not independently explain the economic reason for every individual transaction.

---

## 3. Missing causal variables

A deeper investigation may require additional variables such as:

- customer characteristics
- contract terms
- order-level sales strategy
- competitive pricing
- logistics
- promotional objectives
- product-specific cost drivers

---

## 4. Product comparison

Montana and Carretera are identified as strongest/weakest within the analyzed Enterprise context.

This does not automatically explain why their economics differ.

---

## 5. Discount strategy may be intentional

A high discount could be used to:

- acquire customers
- increase volume
- clear inventory
- enter a market
- respond to competition

Therefore, the business should evaluate whether the resulting margin sacrifice is strategically justified.

---

## 6. Recommendations are analytical priorities

The recommendations should be interpreted as areas for investigation and decision-making, not as guaranteed solutions.

---

# Analytical standard

The project intentionally uses cautious language:

### Strong

> Higher discount intensity is strongly associated with lower Enterprise profitability.

### Too strong

> Higher discounts caused Enterprise's losses.

The first statement is supported by the available analysis; the second requires additional evidence.
